The basics

What is a product?

A product is recurring work, turned into a tool: it packages how your best people do something, so anyone can do it without them.

A report answers once.
A product answers every time.

  • The Monday spreadsheet, the status deck, the email chain: recurring effort, waiting to become a tool.
  • Effort is spent once. A product keeps paying, every time it's used.

Under the hood

LLMs: How they work

Inside the model, every word is a point in space. Meaning is geometry: related words sit close together.

  • Your prompt places the model somewhere in that space.
  • From there it does one thing: predict the next word, again and again.
Next-word prediction

"The client signed the ___" → contract 92% · proposal 7% · bicycle 0.01%

Hover to explore the space. Click a word to see what the model predicts from it. The real space has thousands of dimensions.

Prompt engineering

Prompting is delegation

Talk to it the way you'd brief an engineer: the model performs like the person you brief it to be.

  • Give it a role. Accuracy measurably improves.
  • Give it context. Up to 2x better results on hard tasks.
  • Give it clarity. Every gap gets filled with a guess.

Sources: ExpertPrompting (2023) · EmotionPrompt, Microsoft Research (2023).

The vague ask

"Make me a dashboard."

The clear brief

"You're a senior engineer. Our delivery leads lose track of risks in spreadsheets. Build a one-screen cockpit that flags late projects."

A complete brief has five parts, next.

Clear in, quality out, same as any delegation
Prompt engineering

Prompt Engineering

ROLE“You are a senior engineer building internal tools at Capgemini.”
CONTEXT“Our account leads track client relationships in a mini CRM. Follow-ups slip because nobody knows which account needs attention first.”
TASK“Give each account a 0–100 score for who to call next, with a one-line reason.”
GUIDELINES“Show the score beside each account, sort the list by urgency, plain business language.”
CONSTRAINTS“One screen, use only the data already in the CRM, use the key I provided and store it securely.”
